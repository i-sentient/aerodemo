BRIEF CLINICAL COURSE

Mr. Rao presented with acute anterior chest pain of ~40 minutes. Pre-hospital 12-lead ECG (transmitted en route) was analysed on arrival: anterior OMI, proximal LAD, high confidence, with reciprocal inferior changes. Door-to-balloon pathway activated; ICU bed created via step-down of a stable patient. In the ICU he developed a run of ventricular tachycardia (peri-infarct irritability), detected and escalated immediately; managed with amiodarone and correction of electrolytes, reverted to sinus rhythm. Emergency coronary angiography revealed triple-vessel disease with significant left main stenosis — anatomy unsuitable for PCI. After heart-team discussion and stabilization, he underwent CABG × 4 on Day 3. Post-operative course was uncomplicated; weaned off inotropes by POD-2, extubated POD-1, stepped down POD-4. LV function improved. Discharged in stable condition, ambulant, on guideline-directed medical therapy, with cardiac rehab enrolled.
